## Recommended Portfolio Strategy for SAP Application

### **Lead With (in order):**
1. **Library Web Backend** - Demonstrates backend engineering for enterprise-scale systems
2. **Restaurant System** - Explains understanding of ERP concepts and business operations
3. **Problem Solving** - Shows algorithmic foundation

### **Follow With (based on track):**
- **Analytics Track**: Matrix Decomposition, Matrix Calc v2
- **General Engineering**: Alpha Board Games (demonstrates state/rule management)
- **Innovation Interest**: Facial Recognition (AI/ML direction)

### **Why This Works:**
SAP needs engineers who understand both systems architecture AND business operations. The recommended sequence tells the story:
- "I can build scalable backend systems" (Django)
- "I understand what businesses need from ERP" (Restaurant System)
- "I have strong fundamentals" (Problem Solving)

---

## Key Insights

**Best Single Project**: #5 (Library Web Backend)
- Combines backend engineering, database design, scalability, and enterprise patterns

**Best Combination**: #5 + #4 + #12
- Shows you can build systems, understand business, and solve problems

**Most Strategic Discussion**: Restaurant System → SAP ERP
- Perfect narrative bridge: "This miniature ERP taught me X, which scales to what SAP does globally"

**Avoid Leading With**: Game development, graphics, design-only projects
- Wrong narrative for IT internship focused on enterprise technology
